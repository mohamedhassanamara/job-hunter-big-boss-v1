6 Email Queues

Sends go out one at a time, 15 min apart, from a background process that keeps running even if you close this tab — restarting the app resumes safely without re-sending anything already sent. Cap: 20 items per queue, 500/day safety net (0 sent today).

Email sending isn't configured yet — set GMAIL_ADDRESS and GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD in .env (see .env.example) and restart the app. Queues can still be created and edited, but sends will fail until then. Test your credentials in isolation first with python test_send.py.
